# ENOUGH x Judy Center Coverage — Executive Summary

Maryland's 28 ENOUGH grantees collectively serve 106 census tracts; the state's 79 Judy Center Early Learning Hubs are distributed across 24 counties. This analysis answers a single operational question: **which ENOUGH grantees are good candidates to apply for a Judy Center?** A grantee is a strong candidate when its served tracts contain at least one high-poverty elementary school but no Judy Center is currently sited there. Of the 28 grantees, **14 already have a Judy Center inside one of their served tracts** and **14 do not**. The 14 without coverage are listed first below, ordered by the concentrated-poverty rate (CHILD_CPG_PCT) of the highest-CPG candidate elementary school in their tracts.

## Methodology and caveats

**Data sources.** ENOUGH grantee-to-tract roster supplied by GOC (`2025 Updated ENOUGH Grantee Census Tracts and Zipcodes`). Judy Center master list reconciled from the December 2025 MSDE coordinator PDF, the 2019 MSDE coordinator PDF, and individual LEA pages (`judy_centers_full.csv`, 79 sites). Maryland school points and tract polygons from MDP's `ENOUGH_Act_2025` FeatureServer (Layers 0 and 2), downloaded April 2026.

**Spatial join.** Both layers reprojected to NAD83 / Maryland State Plane (EPSG:26985). Each school assigned its containing tract via `geopandas.sjoin(predicate='within')`. All 1,377 schools resolved to a tract; school JURSCODE matched the GEOID20 county prefix in every case.

**Judy Center to MDP school matching.** Tiered: exact normalized name within county → rapidfuzz `token_sort_ratio >= 85` within county → address-based fallback (catches Judy Centers colocated with schools of a different MDP type). Resolved 74 of 79 sites (69 exact, 4 fuzzy, 1 via address). Five sites are not in the MDP layer:

  - **Eutaw-Marshburn Elementary School** (Baltimore City) — investigate with MSDE/GOC; possible closure, recent rename, or PDF-extraction artifact.
  - **Henry Highland Garnett Elementary School** (Kent) — investigate with MSDE/GOC; possible closure, recent rename, or PDF-extraction artifact.
  - **H.H. Garnet Elementary School** (Prince George's) — investigate with MSDE/GOC; possible closure, recent rename, or PDF-extraction artifact.
  - **Dr. Alvita Jeffers Elementary** (Prince George's) — investigate with MSDE/GOC; possible closure, recent rename, or PDF-extraction artifact.
  - **Carlotta G. Robinson-Jackson Elementary** (Prince George's) — investigate with MSDE/GOC; possible closure, recent rename, or PDF-extraction artifact.

**Concentrated Poverty Grant rate.** `CHILD_CPG_PCT` on MDP's school layer is a three-year FARMS rolling average. The 2025 ENOUGH eligibility threshold is 75% (per MDP's 2025 documentation; the 2024 layer used 80% — worth re-confirming with MDP that the values reloaded under the new threshold). Schools below 65% CPG are flagged _lower priority_ in the recommendation column because they fall below the typical Judy Center poverty profile.

**Caveats.**
- A Judy Center's host elementary school being outside a grantee's tract does not mean the JC and grantee aren't already coordinating. Many BC Judy Centers (Moravia Park, John Ruhrah, Maree G Farring) sit just outside grantee polygons; a follow-up using the school attendance-zone layer (`Layer 1`) would surface JCs whose catchment overlaps a grantee tract even when the school point itself does not.
- The FY24 MSDE board document references 86 Judy Center sites; this analysis uses 79. The 7-row gap is most likely Cecil County's post-2019 expansion (1 → 10 sites) plus pending Prince George's additions. Cecil isn't an ENOUGH county; PG is, so the 3 unresolved PG names above warrant verification.
- One grantee (Leaders Breeders Inc., BC tract 24510260301) has no traditional elementary or combined school in its served tract — only Belair-Edison Charter. Judy Center placement at a charter is policy-dependent and worth a separate MSDE conversation.
- The CHILD_POV_PCT column reflects MDP's tract-level child poverty estimate; ACS vintage is not declared on the layer (likely 2019-2023 5-year). For 2026 ENOUGH eligibility decisions that require Margin of Error and prior-year point estimates, the analysis must join the Census API directly — MDP's tract layer does not currently carry those fields.
- A school's CHILD_CPG_PCT can be substantially higher than its tract's CHILD_POV_PCT (e.g., Belle Grove ES at 88% CPG sits in an Anne Arundel tract reporting 0% child poverty). This is expected: CPG is a 3-year FARMS rolling average for kids enrolled at the school, while tract poverty is the share of children residing in the tract. Open-boundary enrollment, magnet programs, and cross-tract attendance zones routinely create that gap.

**Reproducibility.** `python analyze.py` regenerates every CSV in this directory, this summary, and the sanity-check overlays (`sanity_overview_md.png`, `sanity_overview_bc.png`). MDP layer downloads cache to `.cache/` so re-runs are seconds.
