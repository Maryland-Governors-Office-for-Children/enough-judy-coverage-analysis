ENOUGH × Judy Center coverage

Maryland Governor's Office for Children — which ENOUGH grantees are candidates for a Judy Center?
Refreshed Apr 2026

Dashboard

The question this analyses answers

Maryland's 28 ENOUGH grantees serve 106 census tracts. The state has 92 Judy Center Early Learning Hubs. A grantee is a candidate for a Judy Center when its served tracts contain at least one elementary or combined school but no Judy Center is currently sited there. Per GOC guidance the operational filter is presence of an elementary in a JC-free tract, not a CPG threshold — CPG is shown alongside each candidate as information.

Three categories

Already covered —

Grantees whose served tracts already contain a Judy Center. No new application needed.

Catchment overlap —

No in-tract Judy Center, but a Judy Center's attendance zone overlaps a served tract by ≥ 5%. Coordination opportunity — may not require a new application.

Needs Judy Center —

No in-tract Judy Center and no overlapping catchment. Primary candidates for new Judy Center applications.

Named-overlap exemplars

Four grantees whose names already reference their host Judy Center — these validated the spatial-join correctness during development: City of Cumberland ↔ South Penn Elementary, One Annapolis ↔ Georgetown East Elementary, San Mar ↔ Bester Elementary, Frederick LMB ↔ Waverley / Hillcrest / Butterfly Ridge Elementary.

Methodology

Data sources

  • ENOUGH grantee → census tract roster. Supplied by GOC (2025 Updated ENOUGH Grantee Census Tracts and Zipcodes). 28 grantees serving 106 unique census tracts.
  • Judy Center master list. Pulled from marylandchild.org's Judy Center Hubs Map (the SuperStoreFinder XML feed maintained by Maryland Family Network for MSDE). 92 Judy Centers as of April 2026.
  • School points, attendance zones, tract polygons. Maryland Department of Planning's ENOUGH_Act_2025 FeatureServer, Layers 0 / 1 / 2.

Spatial join

Schools and tracts reprojected to NAD83 / Maryland State Plane (EPSG:26985). Each school assigned its containing tract via geopandas.sjoin(predicate='within'). All 1,377 schools resolved to a tract; school JURSCODE matched the GEOID20 county prefix in every case.

Judy Center → MDP school matching

Tiered, in order of accuracy: exact normalized name match within county → rapidfuzz token_sort_ratio ≥ 85 within county → address-based token_sort_ratio ≥ 90 within county → MarylandChild.org lat/lon point-in-polygon. MarylandChild's published lat/lon has known precision issues (some pins are ~1 km off and land in the wrong census tract), so MDP geometry is preferred when the school exists in MDP's school point layer.

Result: all 92 sites resolved (83 exact, 5 fuzzy, 4 address, 4 lat/lon).

Three disjoint categories

  • In-tract (15 grantees). At least one Judy Center inside a served census tract.
  • Catchment overlap (8 grantees). No in-tract JC, but a JC's attendance zone overlaps a served tract by ≥ 5% of either the tract or the catchment area. School-attendance polygons are MDP Layer 1.
  • Needs Judy Center (5 grantees). Neither an in-tract JC nor a catchment overlap. Primary candidates for a new Judy Center.

CHILD_CPG_PCT (Concentrated Poverty Grant percent)

A three-year FARMS rolling average MDP publishes per school. ENOUGH eligibility uses a 75% threshold, but the operational filter for this analysis is presence of an elementary school in a JC-free grantee tract, not CPG. CPG is shown alongside each candidate so reviewers can prioritise where to start, not as a gate.

Closest-elementary fallback

For grantees in the Needs-JC category whose served tract contains no traditional elementary or combined school (currently only Leaders Breeders Inc., whose single tract houses only Belair-Edison Charter), the pipeline finds the nearest non-JC elementary by point-to-tract-edge distance, capped at 10 km, and surfaces it on the map and in the per-grantee detail.

Caveats

  • The map's purple Judy Center dots represent state-wide context; when a grantee is selected, JCs in their tract upgrade to blue stars and JCs whose catchment overlaps their tracts upgrade to green stars.
  • A few JC catchments span an entire small county (e.g., single-school counties on the Eastern Shore). Those are flagged county-wide and excluded from near-miss reporting because they don't represent meaningful proximity. See catchment_countywide_flags.csv.
  • The CHILD_POV_PCT field on MDP's tract layer doesn't declare its ACS 5-year vintage. Likely 2019-2023. For 2026 ENOUGH eligibility decisions that require Margin of Error and prior-year point estimates, the analysis must join the Census API directly.
  • Some MarylandChild.org lat/lon pins are off (e.g., Georgetown East ES is pinned in Chesapeake Bay). The pipeline mitigates by preferring MDP's authoritative coordinates.

Reproducibility

python analyze.py regenerates every CSV in the project root, this site's data files, and the executive summary markdown. MDP layers and the MarylandChild.org feed cache to .cache/ so re-runs are fast.
