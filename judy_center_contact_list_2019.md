# Judy Center Locations / Contact List

**Source:** MSDE Judy Center Coordinator List, revised 7/30/2019.
**Original distribution:** Maryland State Department of Education, Office of Early Childhood.
**Authoritative contact:** Amy Beal, MSDE — 410-767-0675 — amy.beal@maryland.gov
**Web reference:** http://earlychildhood.marylandpublicschools.org/families/judy-centers

This file is a faithful conversion of the 2019 PDF into markdown. It preserves the original site list, addresses, principals, and coordinator contacts as of summer 2019. **It does not reflect post-2019 expansion** under the Blueprint for Maryland's Future legislation (which committed to ~9 new sites/year FY21-25 and ~18/year FY26-30). The current FY24/FY25 site count is approximately 86. Use `judy_centers_full.csv` for a reconciled current-as-possible list; use this file as the verified 2019 baseline.
